HELLO, NOISE MAKER

A playground
with a pulse.

  1. Press play. All six voices synthesize in your browser. Pick a universe from the preset menu.
  2. Make it yours. Tap steps to switch notes on and off. Hold a step to change its note, velocity, probability, and ratchets. M mutes, S solos, and the lock protects a voice from generation.
  3. Turn the machine. Drag knobs up or down, scroll them, or use arrow keys. Double-tap a knob to reset it. EVOLVE gently changes unlocked voices; CHAIN switches scenes every four bars.
  4. Patch something. Tap a source jack, then a destination. Cables really modulate sound. Tap the × beside a connection to unplug it.
  5. Get a little weird. Drag the XY pad or hold a performance pad. Feed the Cloud your own audio for granular textures.
  6. Keep it. Render a WAV loop, record a live take, or download your session.
